Program overview

The rapid adoption of Generative AI is redefining roles in software engineering, data science, cloud computing, and product development. This program prepares Computer Science students to integrate AI tools into technical workflows such as coding, debugging, documentation, problem-solving, and career preparation. Learners gain hands-on experience with Generative AI platforms to enhance productivity, communication, analytical thinking, and professional readiness in AI-augmented software careers.

01

Building job-readiness skills in the AI era

6 hours

In today’s AI-driven job market, adaptability and evolving skill sets are crucial for professional success. This module explores the fundamental employability skills required to thrive in AI-augmented workplaces, distinguishing them from general life skills. Learners will develop problem-solving, critical thinking, and emotional intelligence while understanding industry expectations, workplace ethics, and effective communication for collaboration. By the end, participants will be better equipped to align their skill sets with evolving job roles such as software developer, QA engineer, DevOps engineer, and data analyst and workplace demands.

02

Smart work with Gen AI for professional excellence

10 hours

Generative AI is revolutionizing how professionals work by automating routine tasks and enhancing efficiency. This module delves into AI-powered productivity tools, enabling learners to enhance coding productivity through intelligent documentation, test-case generation, debugging assistance, code explanation, AI-assisted research, report writing, email drafting, and data analysis. Ethical AI use, decision-making support, and AI-assisted research methodologies are explored to help professionals optimize workflows while maintaining accuracy and integrity. Participants will gain hands-on experience with AI tools, making them more effective and competitive in their fields.

03

Optimizing AI interaction: the art of prompt engineering

10 hours

Interacting effectively with AI requires mastering the skill of crafting precise and structured prompts to generate accurate responses and obtain reliable outputs for coding support, API documentation, error resolution, algorithm design, and system explanations. Learners gain experience in refining prompts, debugging model responses, controlling outputs, and using AI tools for brainstorming software solutions and automation scripts. This module provides hands-on training in designing prompts that maximize AI’s capabilities while minimizing errors. Learners will explore how AI models interpret instructions, refine AI-generated outputs, and use AI tools for brainstorming, content creation, and automation. With a focus on optimizing communication with AI, participants will enhance their ability to leverage AI for various professional applications.

04

AI-powered career development

8 hours

Generative AI can sharpen every step of a job search. This module shows learners how to use AI tools to build and refine resumes, cover letters, GitHub profiles, and LinkedIn branding, prepare for interviews, and network with confidence. Learners map their skills to roles such as software developer, QA engineer, DevOps engineer, and data analyst, and plan their next career steps with AI support.

05

AI applications in workplace scenarios

6 hours

AI is increasingly shaping workplace collaboration, teamwork, and business decision-making across industries. This module provides practical insights into AI-powered tools for enhancing workplace efficiency, including meeting summarization, automated transcriptions, and data-driven decision support. Learners will analyze real-world industry-specific AI applications, exploring how AI aids market research, competitive analysis, and business strategy development. Also, learners explore how AI integrates into agile workflows, DevOps pipelines, and cross-functional team collaboration. By understanding AI’s role in different sectors, participants will gain a competitive edge in integrating AI into their professional environments.

Capstone project: AI-driven job readiness portfolio

Throughout the program, participants will apply their AI-driven skills to build a comprehensive job readiness portfolio, integrating AI tools for resume enhancement, cover letter optimization, and interview preparation. The project culminates in a final presentation where learners showcase their AI-augmented career strategy. Industry mentors and peers will provide feedback, helping participants refine their portfolios and improve their job market readiness. This hands-on project ensures learners leave the program with tangible, AI-enhanced career assets, ready to make a strong impression in their professional journeys.

This program provides a structured approach to developing job readiness in the Generative AI era. It combines foundational knowledge, practical application, and hands-on experience to prepare learners for the evolving job market. By the end of the curriculum, participants will have a well-rounded understanding of AI tools, employability skills, and career readiness strategies essential for success in an AI-driven workforce.
