What you learn, day by day

Each day has a morning and an afternoon session. Pick a day to see its topics and what you will be able to do at the end of it.

Industrial Pneumatic Control

8 hours

Morning session

  • Fundamentals of compressed air supply: production, preparation, and distribution.
  • Symbolic representation: ISO 1219 standards for device symbols.
  • Actuators: cylinder construction, operation, and applications.

Afternoon session

  • Valves: identification, construction, and operation of direction control valves and other control elements.
  • Control techniques: position control (limit switches and sensors), speed control (flow control valves), logic control (AND/OR valves), pressure control (adjustable pressure sequence valve), and time-dependent control (timers and counters).

Outcomes: select the correct components, read and design basic pneumatic circuits, and troubleshoot pneumatic systems.

Advanced Industrial Pneumatic Control

8 hours

Morning session

  • Sequential multi-cylinder control: basics of sequential control and signal overlap.
  • Motion and control diagrams: techniques for plotting and identifying signal overlap.

Afternoon session

  • Cascade principle: sequential multi-cylinder cascade control by grouping methods.
  • Stepper sequencer principle: advanced sequential control using expansion modules.
  • Vacuum generation: vacuum systems and their accessories for control applications.

Outcomes: awareness of advanced pneumatics, ability to develop and troubleshoot multi-cylinder circuits, and application of vacuum in control systems.

Industrial Electro-Pneumatic Control

8 hours

Morning session

  • Fundamentals of electro-pneumatic control: control chain and electric symbols (contacts, switches, relays, timers, and counters).
  • Solenoid-operated valves: construction, working principles, and applications.

Afternoon session

  • Sensors: magnetic, inductive, capacitive, optical, and pressure sensors and their applications.
  • Graphical symbols interpretation: symbols in electrical circuits and on electro-pneumatic components.

Outcomes: design, assemble, and troubleshoot basic electro-pneumatic circuits, maintain control systems, and read electrical control circuits.

Electro-Pneumatic Automation with PLC

8 hours

Morning session

  • PLC fundamentals: PLC architecture, programming environments, and control chain in electro-pneumatic systems.
  • Sensor integration: connecting sensors to PLCs based on specifications.

Afternoon session

  • PLC programming: developing PLC logic control using electro-pneumatic elements.
  • Commissioning: communicating with and commissioning PLC systems from a PC.

Outcomes: use electro-pneumatics, sensors, and PLCs in control systems, develop and troubleshoot PLC-controlled electro-pneumatic systems, and automate controls with PLC programming.

Simulation Software and Review

8 hours

Morning session

  • Industrial simulation software: basics of simulation software for designing and testing automated systems.
  • Virtual commissioning: hands-on exercises in using simulation software to commission and optimize mechatronic systems.

Afternoon session

  • Case studies and review: analysis of real-world applications and a Q&A session to consolidate learning.

Outcomes: simulate, design, and troubleshoot automated systems in a virtual environment.

Participants gain extensive knowledge and practical skills in pneumatic and electro-pneumatic systems, advanced pneumatics, PLC integration, and industrial simulation software. The mix of theory and hands-on practice prepares learners for real-world challenges in automation and control systems.
